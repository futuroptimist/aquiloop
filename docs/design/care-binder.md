# Home, garden, and aquarium care binder design brief

**Status:** reference and layout brief; concept approval and implementation pending

**Revision:** 2026-09-14

## Purpose and sequence

This brief is the authority for a directly printable care binder. It prepares a
layout comparison; it does **not** approve a concept or specify final care for a
particular specimen. Work proceeds in this order:

1. approve one of the three visual directions below (or a revision of one);
2. collect photographs of the user's actual specimens and site measurements;
3. verify identities and specimen-specific care;
4. implement and test the PDF; and
5. inspect rendered pages, then a physical print.

The first combined PDF has exactly six US Letter pages in this order:

1. Sedum reported on the nursery label as “Love's Fire” (Lowe's; outdoor grow bag);
2. *Kalanchoe humilis* reported as “Desert Surprise” (Lowe's; outdoor grow bag);
3. pothos (indoors; provisionally *Epipremnum aureum*);
4. bird of paradise (indoors initially; species unresolved);
5. aquarium hornwort (provisionally *Ceratophyllum demersum*); and
6. a handwritten watering and aquarium-maintenance log.

Pages 1–5 are each independently limited to one page. A possible later
indoor/outdoor bird-of-paradise comparison is not part of the initial manifest.

## Comparable concept directions

Every concept uses the same content density: one macro specimen photograph;
common name plus binomial; eight short category blocks; one optional small
diagram; and a compact sources/revision footer. This constraint makes the later
comparison about composition rather than missing content.

### A. Botanical field guide

A calm reference plate with a large, naturally lit macro at top, serif display
name, italic binomial, fine rules, restrained botanical color, and two balanced
columns of concise entries. A small line detail may explain a diagnostic leaf or
root feature when identification benefits.

place a crisp two-column grid with exactly eight labeled blocks: Identity,
Origin & Habit, Light, Soil, Water, Temperature, Feeding & Maintenance, and
Troubleshooting & Trivia. Add one tiny botanical line-detail inset and a compact

generous left punch margin, printer-safe margins, realistic readable placeholder
sentences rather than decorative gibberish, no extra plants, no hands, no binder,
no perspective, no page shadow.

## Page and content contract

### Physical and typographic specification

- Page: US Letter portrait, 8.5 × 11 in (612 × 792 PostScript points).
- Safe area: 0.85 in at the bound/left edge for a three-hole punch; 0.5 in top,
  right, and bottom. Keep live content out of those margins; backgrounds need
  not bleed. The resulting live area is 7.15 × 10 in.
- Type: body 10.5 pt with at least 13 pt leading; labels 9 pt semibold; captions
  and references at least 8 pt with 10 pt leading; page title 24–30 pt; binomial
  13–16 pt italic. Never shrink body text merely to satisfy pagination.
- Use near-black text on white or very light backgrounds and target WCAG-style
  contrast of at least 4.5:1 for normal text. Meaning must survive grayscale:
  combine color with labels, patterns, rules, or shapes.
- The macro photograph should occupy approximately 35–40% of the live area and
  have enough source resolution for its final size (target 300 ppi; reject
  visibly soft, clipped, or poorly exposed files during inspection).

### Profile schema

Each category has one dedicated, clearly labeled grid item. Copy remains concise:

1. **Identity:** reported common/nursery name, verified binomial and authority,
   family, confidence, and diagnostic evidence.
2. **Origin & growth habit:** native range versus horticultural provenance;
   mature form without claiming a specimen measurement.
3. **Light & exposure:** intensity/location, acclimation steps, and stress cues.
4. **Soil/substrate:** drainage and practical components by volume only when a
   reliable source or an explicitly labeled starting recipe supports them.
5. **Water:** observable trigger, thorough/safe method, and a conditional range;
   never a calendar promise.
6. **Temperature & seasons:** supported range, hazards, and seasonal response.
7. **Feeding & routine maintenance:** conditional feeding, pruning/cleaning,
   propagation where useful, and toxicity/safety where supported.
8. **Troubleshooting & trivia:** a short symptom/action list plus one sourced
   species- or genus-level fact; do not present genus trivia as species fact.

The footer contains compact numbered sources, revision date, profile version,
and unresolved-evidence flags. Optional diagrams are allowed only for a useful
diagnostic feature, pruning/propagation cut, soil-layer misconception, light
orientation, or aquatic placement—not as decoration.

For hornwort, replace terrestrial categories rather than forcing them: aquarium
placement and floating/anchoring behavior; light; water temperature and relevant
parameters; nutrient/CO2 context; trimming and fragment control; shedding,
competition, and livestock compatibility. “Soil” becomes **substrate/placement**
and “watering” becomes **water conditions & aquarium maintenance**.

## Identity and evidence rules

The nursery label is reported evidence, not a taxonomic conclusion. Store and
display separately: (a) label text and seller, (b) accepted botanical name and
authority, (c) cultivar epithet, (d) trademark/marketing name, and (e) plant
patent or application claim with number and status **as of a dated check**.
Never copy “PPAF” forward as timeless identity text.

### Evidence classes and missing inputs

- **Broadly supported principle:** multiple botanical authorities, extension
  services, botanic gardens, or an identifiable grower agree. State scope.
- **Suggested starting point:** a credible source gives a usable recipe or range,
  but conditions vary. Label it “start here, then observe,” with provenance.
- **Local adaptation:** derived from logged results at the actual site. Date it.
- **Unknown:** leave a visible placeholder; never manufacture precision.

Before final care writing, collect original, high-resolution specimen and label
photos plus: grow-bag dimensions; the existing soil/additives and approximate
volumes; drainage; compass exposure and hours of direct light; watering/rain
history; indoor window direction, light and temperature; observed symptoms; and
the aquarium information listed above. “Pacifica, California” is prior context,
not a measurement of either growing site. Local weather is not a substitute for
site exposure, pot moisture, indoor climate, or aquarium readings.

## Handwritten log contract

Page 6 is a landscape-style table composed within the same Letter portrait page:

- Fixed left columns: **date/time** (1.0 in) and **event/weather notes** (1.25 in).
- Five equal plant columns fill the remaining 4.9 in: Sedum, Kalanchoe, pothos,
  bird of paradise, and hornwort. Each header has a short verified display name,
  location cue, and an interval field.
- Terrestrial header format: “Approx. interval: ___ days — check moisture, plant
  condition, season, and weather first.” Values remain blank in layout concepts.
  Hornwort reads “Watering: N/A — record aquarium maintenance.”
- Each plant cell accepts amount **or** method plus a brief observation; a plant
  not tended during that dated event remains blank. Do not use checkmarks that
  imply every plant received water.
- Provide 10 writable rows, each at least 0.68 in tall. The notes column explicitly
  invites rain amount/observation for the two outdoor plants. Use 8.5 pt minimum
  header text and rules dark enough to photocopy without overwhelming handwriting.

If usability testing shows the five plant cells cannot hold the required notes,
reduce row count before reducing row height or type size.

## Future implementation and verification contract

### Recommended toolchain

Use **LuaLaTeX** with a small data-driven TeX layer (for example, structured TeX
records read by one profile template). It provides exact physical dimensions,
strong typography, stable pagination controls, local OpenType fonts, mature image
handling, and a straightforward headless build. Pin the TeX Live release and all
non-system tooling in build documentation. Installation, source templates, fonts,
assets, and dependencies are deliberately deferred.

### Build contract

- Keep one machine-readable ordered manifest as the only assembly order. Every
  item declares a stable ID, type (`profile` or `supplement`), source file, output
  title, and explicit `page_budget`. The initial budgets are `1` for each of the
  five profiles and `1` for the log; future multipage entries must opt into an
  integer budget greater than one in the entry itself and manifest review.
- Produce one combined PDF, exactly six pages initially, in manifest order. Also
  compile each profile in isolation and require its declared budget; concatenated
  total count alone cannot detect one overflowing entry offset by another failure.
- Store only appropriately licensed local photographs, diagrams, and font files
  (or deterministic system packages); prohibit network fetches during the build.
  Document asset credits, licenses, checksums where useful, commands, engine and
  dependency versions. Do not commit generated PDFs.
- GitHub Actions builds and validates on pull requests that change binder inputs
  or tooling, matching changes on `main`, and `workflow_dispatch`. It uploads one
  clearly named downloadable combined-PDF artifact. No credential, release,
  website, or publishing service is required.

### Automated acceptance tests

1. fail on missing manifest entries, content fields, local assets/fonts, source
   citations, or unresolved placeholders accidentally marked final;
2. build every entry alone and compare page count with its entry-level budget;
3. build the combined document and assert manifest order, exactly six pages, and
   every page's MediaBox is 612 × 792 pt (allow only a documented numeric tolerance);
4. detect empty/near-empty pages, text or image bounds outside the live area,
   overset/overflow warnings, missing glyphs, low-resolution images, and build
   log errors;
5. include a fixture that adds enough real text to one profile to force a second
   page, and assert the entry-budget check fails with that profile's ID. The test
   must still fail if another manifest item is removed, proving it is not merely
   a six-page total assertion; and
6. rebuild from a clean checkout and compare structural metadata or normalized
   output, accounting explicitly for timestamps rather than asserting a fragile
   byte match without normalization.

### Human acceptance gates

Render every page to images at a defined DPI and inspect at 100% for clipping,
overflow, missing glyphs, name hierarchy, readable body/footer type, photo crop
and sharpness, grayscale distinctions, left punch clearance, and comfortable log
writing space. Then print on an ordinary printer at 100% scale, punch a sacrificial
copy, write a full sample row, view it under expected lighting, and record approval.
Passing automation alone does not make the binder print-worthy.
