\CardRow{Identity first}{Record photographs without chasing or handling the fish. Keep the working name Pangio spp.; similar trade fish can be confused. No specimen photograph or species determination is supplied. \textbf{[KUH-SF]}}{Temperature}{Trade-group guide: 74--80 F (23--27 C, source rounding). A care range, not today's measurement or a heater-change instruction. See the next page for source differences. \textbf{[KUH-COOP]}}
\CardRow{Company}{Guides recommend groups: Co-Op 3--6 or more; Seriously Fish 5--6 minimum for P. semicincta. The current pair merits a welfare review, not an automatic purchase. \textbf{[KUH-COOP, KUH-SF]}}{Space review}{Co-Op discusses 20 gallons or larger. Measure usable bottom area, cover and other stock before a group decision; gallon volume alone does not establish capacity. Quarantine planning comes first. \textbf{[KUH-COOP, KUH-VET]}}
\CardRow{Feed the fish}{Deliberately offer sinking food and suitable varied frozen/live foods. Observe access after lights dim if other animals take it first. Scavenging leftovers is not a feeding plan. \textbf{[KUH-COOP, KUH-LOL]}}{Size and age}{Trade-guide adult size: 3--4 inches. Residence of about three years is not the fish's age. Individual lifespan and age at maturity remain unknown for this unidentified stock. \textbf{[KUH-COOP]}}
\Panel{Proposed routine / observe without dismantling shelter}{Keep a brief record of feeding access, body condition and behavior. A hidden fish is not automatically sick; a change from its usual pattern deserves attention. Check water and equipment promptly if breathing, appetite or balance changes, and seek aquatic veterinary advice for persistent signs. Do not medicate from a page alone. \textbf{[KUH-VET; proposed application]}}
\Panel{Before considering more animals}{Record footprint, all occupants and water results; review the pair's health and social needs with a knowledgeable keeper. No purchase, transfer or tank alteration is prescribed by this draft. The current setup is reported, not measured.}
