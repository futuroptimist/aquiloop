\CompanionHeader{TANK SETUP}{Kuhli loaches}{Pangio spp. / species-scoped water evidence}{Tank conditions govern care. Pacifica weather and a tank's age are not water measurements.}
\Context{Published ranges disagree. Seriously Fish's P. semicincta profile: 21--26 C (about 70--79 F), pH 3.5--7.0, GH 0--8 dGH. These are broad reference ranges, not home setpoints. Never target pH 3.5 or zero hardness from this table.}
\CardRow{Keep scopes separate}{Loaches Online's P. semicincta account instead gives 25.5--28 C (78--82 F), pH 6.2--7.0 and soft water. Confirm identity and stock history before choosing a stable target. \textbf{[KUH-LOL]}}{Measure, then plan}{Current temperature, pH, GH and KH are unknown. No numeric KH target is established here. Test tank and replacement water; avoid abrupt changes to established fish water. \textbf{[KUH-VET; proposed]}}
\CardRow{Bottom and refuge}{Soft sand, shaded hiding areas and secure decor support bottom use. The P. semicincta guide gives at least 60 x 30 cm base; this is a minimum reference, not a stocking formula. \textbf{[KUH-SF]}}{Flow and escape routes}{Provide gentle circulation, surface movement, protected filter intakes and a secure cover. An air stone's presence does not quantify oxygen or safe flow. Inspect gaps before additions. \textbf{[KUH-SF]}}
\Panel{Plant options / proposed habitat plan}{Retain healthy hornwort and guppy grass with intake clearance. Java moss offers fine cover; Java fern and Cryptocoryne suit the shaded habitat guidance. Anubias nana is an optional compact shelter choice, adapted from the Co-Op's Anubias-root example. Leave feeding access and swimming routes. Separate plant entries and attachment instructions are future work. \textbf{[KUH-SF, KUH-MOSS, KUH-COOP]}}
\Panel{Water and biosecurity gate}{Before additions, require undetectable ammonia/nitrite on working tests; record nitrate trend and units. No species-specific nitrate ceiling is established here. Preserve biological filtration. Veterinary guidance recommends separate quarantine with dedicated equipment for at least 30 days, sometimes longer. Treatment choices require fish and invertebrate compatibility review. \textbf{[KUH-VET]}}
