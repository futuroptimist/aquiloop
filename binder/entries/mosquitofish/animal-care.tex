\CardRow{Light and shelter}{Some sun, submerged refuge and open feeding water; adjust shade to measured heat. Avoid shallow exposed containers and dense permanent shade. [MF-SM; MF-MO]}{Food access}{Natural food varies. San Mateo discourages routine feeding in productive ponds; San Joaquin permits modest flakes when food is scarce. Observe condition; remove leftovers and reduce with cold appetite. [MF-SM; MF-SJ]}
\Panel{Daily / weekly / after change}{Daily: behavior, feeding access, water level, temperature, flow and casualties. Weekly and after weather or equipment changes: chemistry, population, screens and vegetation. Preserve biofilter media; use conditioned, temperature-matched partial changes guided by tests. [MF-VET; MF-UF; proposed]}
\Panel{Stop and review}{Rapid gills with persistent gulping, imbalance, injuries or deaths require prompt water/equipment checks and district or aquatic-vet help. Surface feeding alone is not a diagnosis. Do not assume safety with shrimp, Kuhli loaches or native amphibians; review biosecurity and product history. [MF-FWS; MF-VET]}
