\CareRow{LIGHT / EXPOSURE}{Moderate to bright light; avoid direct sun. Prolonged low light may reduce variegation or foliage brightness. Exposure/cultivar remain unknown. \textbf{[POT-PSU]}}{SOIL / SUBSTRATE}{Use a well-aerated, well-drained houseplant medium and open drainage holes. These sources do not prescribe an ingredient recipe. \textbf{[POT-WISC; POT-NCSU]}}
\CareRow{WATER}{When the surface is dry, water the root ball thoroughly; let excess drain and empty the saucer. Recheck moisture, not a calendar; persistent wetness promotes root rot. \textbf{[POT-WISC; POT-NCSU]}}{TEMPERATURE / SEASON}{Keep away from drafts. When light/cooler conditions slow growth, follow active growth and slower dry-down—not calendar winter as unconditional dormancy. \textbf{[POT-WISC]}}
\CareRow{FEEDING / MAINTENANCE}{Feed periodically while actively growing; reduce or pause when growth slows. Wipe dusty leaves; prune long stems for a fuller habit. \textbf{[POT-WISC; POT-NCSU]}}{PROPAGATION}{Use a stem piece with node, leaf, and bud—not a leaf alone. In medium: node/bud covered, leaf exposed. In water: node submerged, foliage above water. \textbf{[POT-NCSU-PROP; POT-WISC]}}
\CareRow{TROUBLESHOOTING}{Root rot → excess water; droop → too little; scorch/yellow → direct sun plausible. Chewing is toxic to pets/people; sap may irritate skin. \textbf{[POT-PSU; POT-WISC; POT-NCSU]}}{NATURAL HISTORY / TRIVIA}{Candidate \textit{E. aureum} is a climbing/trailing evergreen aroid native to the Society Islands; it climbs with aerial roots. \textbf{[POT-NCSU]}}
