\CareRow{LIGHT / EXPOSURE}{Moderate to bright light; avoid direct sun. Prolonged low light may reduce variegation. \textbf{[POT-PSU]}}{SOIL / SUBSTRATE}{Use loose, well-aerated, well-drained houseplant medium in a pot with a drainage hole. Sources support qualities, not a recipe ratio. \textbf{[POT-NCSU; POT-WISC]}}
\CareRow{WATER}{When the soil surface is dry, water the whole root ball thoroughly and let excess drain; do not leave it saturated or water by calendar. \textbf{[POT-WISC; POT-PSU]}}{TEMPERATURE / SEASON}{Keep away from drafts. Calendar winter does not prove dormancy: adjust care from current growth and medium dry-down. \textbf{[POT-WISC]}}
\CareRow{FEEDING / MAINTENANCE}{Feed periodically while actively growing; reduce/pause when growth slows. Prune long vines. Keep from chewing pets; sap can irritate skin. \textbf{[POT-WISC; POT-ASPCA]}}{PROPAGATION}{Cut a stem with node, leaf, and bud—not a leaf alone. Put the node/bud in water or medium; keep foliage above it. Pot after roots form. \textbf{[POT-NCSU-PROP; POT-WISC]}}
\CareRow{TROUBLESHOOTING}{Root rot → saturation plausible; droop → dryness plausible; yellowing → direct sun plausible. Check moisture and exposure first. \textbf{[POT-PSU]}}{NATURAL HISTORY / TRIVIA}{\textit{E. aureum} is an evergreen Society Islands aroid; aerial roots support its climbing habit. \textbf{[POT-NCSU]}}
