\CompanionHeader{REPRODUCTION}{Cherry shrimp}{Neocaridina davidi / freshwater colony planning}{Direct development: miniature freshwater hatchlings, no saline larval nursery. [CS-UF]}
\Context{A fish-free nursery must be ready before breeding. Community-tank plants cannot guarantee juvenile safety. The proposed nursery and later adult transfer remain separate decisions.}
\Step{1}{Decide and plan capacity}{Choose whether breeding is wanted; arrange suitable long-term homes and spare capacity before expansion. Never release shrimp or aquarium contents outdoors. [CS-UF; proposed]}
\Step{2}{Prepare the nursery}{Verify biofiltration, stable prepared water, grazing surfaces, sheltered molts and protected intakes. A tank's age or one clean test is not readiness evidence. [CS-VET; CS-FARM]}
\Step{3}{Establish healthy adults}{Both sexes are needed; a purchase count guarantees neither. Adult female body shape and a saddle are clues, not color-only juvenile sexing. A saddle is developing ovarian eggs; berried means eggs carried below the abdomen. [CS-COOP; CS-PAN]}
\Step{4}{Observe eggs without handling}{Record when carried eggs first appear. The laboratory interval was 16--19 days at 25 $\pm$ 1 C; Co-Op describes about a month. Keep estimates separate, without a forced hatch deadline. [CS-PAN; CS-COOP]}
\Step{5}{Support hatchlings}{About 1--2 mm hatchlings need sheltered grazing and modest accessible fine food. Preserve biofilm, protect intakes and avoid excess powder. Record feeding and growth separately from first sighting. [CS-UF; CS-FARM]}
\Step{6}{Separate developmental milestones}{At 25 $\pm$ 1 C, ovarian development began around day 30 (mean 36.6); the first embryo-carrying female appeared at day 49 (mean 55.18). These are distinct laboratory endpoints, not a home maturity timetable. Do not heat to hurry breeding: growth-phase females maturing and mating at 32 C lost eggs in another experiment. [CS-PAN; CS-TRO]}
\Step{7}{Review transfers and stop cues}{Before later adult transfer, review both systems' water, stock, footprint, health, acclimation and a fallback home. No safe transfer size or count is known. Kuhli keeper reports are not predation trials; juveniles and fresh molts remain vulnerable. Pause expansion/transfer for unusual losses, persistent failure to feed, molt problems or unstable water; review equipment, products and health. [CS-VET; CS-KUH; CS-SF; inference]}
