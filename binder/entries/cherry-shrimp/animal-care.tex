\Context{Daniel is considering a shrimp-only 10-gallon nursery and later adult transfers to the existing 20-gallon Kuhli tank. Neither plan establishes cycling, capacity or compatibility.}
\CardRow{Temperature}{Co-Op prefers 72--76 F (22--24 C). Its broad 60--82 F (16--28 C) range is not a setpoint or permission for rapid swings. Celsius values are rounded. \textbf{[CS-COOP]}}{Grazing surfaces}{Shrimp browse biofilm and organic material. A newly filled tank may lack food. Grazing does not replace filtration or planned feeding. \textbf{[CS-UF; CS-COOP]}}
\CardRow{Feed and observe}{Proposed: small varied shrimp-food meals; check access and remove excess. Offer fine food sparingly near juvenile cover. No universal ration is established. \textbf{[CS-FARM; CS-COOP]}}{Molting and shelter}{Growth requires shedding the shell; cast shells may be eaten. Soft shrimp need refuge. A molt problem alone does not diagnose a mineral deficiency; test water and review diet first. \textbf{[CS-UF; CS-COOP; proposed]}}
\Panel{Medicine review}{Review treatment history and proposed products. Copper medication can harm invertebrates and biofiltration. Fish-safe does not establish shrimp-safe; seek aquatic veterinary advice for illness. \textbf{[CS-VET; proposed]}}
\Panel{Observe the animals, not just the colony count}{Proposed: record grazing, food access, molts, condition and unusual losses; check water/equipment for changes. Individual lifespan is unknown here. Before stocking, confirm supplier identity/water, health and nursery readiness; a continuing colony is not one shrimp's lifespan.}
