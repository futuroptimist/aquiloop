\CareRow{LIGHT / EXPOSURE}{Strong light; acclimate gradually to more direct sun. Check for scorch or stretched growth rather than assuming the grow-bag site is suitable. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{SOIL / SUBSTRATE}{Use a freely draining succulent medium and a draining container. The current “soil plus additives” recipe is unknown. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}
\CareRow{WATER}{Water thoroughly only after the medium dries. Recheck after rain; observed dry-down overrides any calendar interval. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{TEMPERATURE / SEASON}{Unless hardiness is established, protect the grow bag from frost. In colder, darker, slower growth, keep drier; avoid cold plus wet. No local threshold is set. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}
\CareRow{FEEDING / MAINTENANCE}{During active spring/summer growth, feed lightly with low nitrogen; succulents need less fertilizer than many houseplants. Stop when winter growth slows. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{PROPAGATION}{Cut a healthy stem with clean, sharp pruners. Let the end dry and callus; place in well-drained medium. Wetness before rooting raises rot risk. \textbf{[SED-IA]}}
\CareRow{TROUBLESHOOTING}{Spindly growth or pale, shrivelled, dropping leaves are cues, not diagnoses. Check actual light and dry-down; overwatering or poor drainage can promote root rot. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{NATURAL HISTORY / TRIVIA}{The patent says LOVE’S FIRE was selected in 2022 as a mutation of ‘Strawberry’; this applies to the patented cultivar, not yet this specimen. \textbf{[SED-PAT]}}
