\CareRow{LIGHT / EXPOSURE}{Strong light; acclimate gradually to more direct sun. Check for scorch or stretched growth rather than assuming the grow-bag site is suitable. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{SOIL / SUBSTRATE}{Use a commercial freely draining succulent medium in a container with open drainage. No retained source supports one universal ingredient ratio; do not assume an existing grow-bag recipe. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}
\CareRow{WATER}{Starting cue: water thoroughly only after the medium dries. Recheck after rain; observation overrides any calendar interval. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{TEMPERATURE / SEASON}{Protect this provisional tender succulent from frost; reduce water as light and growth decline. Exact outdoor thresholds are unknown. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}
\CareRow{FEEDING / MAINTENANCE}{Feed sparingly during active growth; withhold when growth slows. Remove damaged tissue only with clean tools. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{PROPAGATION}{Healthy stem or whole leaf cutting: callus the wound, then use well-drained medium. Excess moisture before rooting risks rot. \textbf{[SED-IA]}}
\CareRow{TROUBLESHOOTING}{Soft/translucent tissue → moisture or cold plausible → check drainage, moisture, temperatures. Stretching → low light plausible → verify exposure; acclimate gradually. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{NATURAL HISTORY / TRIVIA}{The patent says LOVE’S FIRE was selected in 2022 as a mutation of ‘Strawberry’; this applies to the patented cultivar, not yet this specimen. \textbf{[SED-PAT]}}
