\CareRow{LIGHT / EXPOSURE}{Strong light; acclimate gradually to more direct sun. Check for scorch or stretched growth rather than assuming the grow-bag site is suitable. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{SOIL / SUBSTRATE}{Use a freely draining succulent medium and a draining container. The current “soil plus additives” recipe is unknown. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}
\CareRow{WATER}{Water thoroughly after the medium dries; let excess drain. In the outdoor grow bag, recheck after rain and follow observed dry-down—not a calendar. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{TEMPERATURE / SEASON}{Protect from frost. As colder, darker conditions slow growth, keep the medium drier; no unsourced temperature threshold is assigned. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}
\CareRow{FEEDING / MAINTENANCE}{Feed lightly only during active growth; reduce or stop when growth slows. Check outdoor plants more often as sun and weather change water use. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{PROPAGATION}{Take a healthy stem cutting with clean, sharp pruners; remove lower leaves. Let the cut end dry and callus before placing it in well-drained medium; earlier placement raises rot risk. \textbf{[SED-IA]}}
\CareRow{TROUBLESHOOTING}{Spindly growth → low light plausible → verify exposure before increasing sun gradually. Persistent wetness/poor drainage → root-rot risk → check bag, medium, and rain. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{NATURAL HISTORY / TRIVIA}{The patent says LOVE’S FIRE was selected in 2022 as a mutation of ‘Strawberry’; this applies to the patented cultivar, not yet this specimen. \textbf{[SED-PAT]}}
