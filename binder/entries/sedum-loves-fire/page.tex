\CareRow{LIGHT / EXPOSURE}{Strong light. Outdoors, begin in shade and increase light over 10–14 days to limit permanent succulent sun injury. \textbf{[SED-MSU; SED-IA-OUT]}}{SOIL / SUBSTRATE}{Use succulent mix, or a freely draining blend containing coarse sand, potting mix, and perlite, in a draining pot. No sourced ratio is available. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}
\CareRow{WATER}{After the medium dries, water thoroughly until water drains; recheck after rain rather than following a calendar. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{TEMPERATURE / SEASON}{Protect this tender-succulent example from frost; keep cold dormant plants comparatively dry and adjust water as light/growth decline. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}
\CareRow{FEEDING / MAINTENANCE}{Feed sparingly during active growth; pause while growth is slow. Use clean tools for damaged tissue. \textbf{[SED-MSU]}}{PROPAGATION}{Take a healthy stem or whole leaf cutting; callus the wound, then use well-drained medium. Too much moisture before rooting risks rot. \textbf{[SED-IA]}}
\CareRow{TROUBLESHOOTING}{Soft/translucent tissue → wet or cold plausible → check drainage, moisture, temperatures.
