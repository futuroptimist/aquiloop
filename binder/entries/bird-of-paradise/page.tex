\CareRow{LIGHT / EXPOSURE}{\textit{S. reginae}: sunny indoors. \textit{S. nicolai}: bright indirect light. Do not combine these into one target. \textbf{[BOP-REG; BOP-NIC]}}{SOIL / SUBSTRATE}{\textit{S. reginae}: fertile, loamy, well-drained soil. \textit{S. nicolai}: moist, well-drained loam and sand. Qualitative examples; no sourced ratio. \textbf{[BOP-REG; BOP-NIC]}}
\CareRow{WATER}{\textit{S. reginae}: freely spring/summer, drier winter. \textit{S. nicolai}: when the top layer feels dry. Drain excess; no fixed interval. \textbf{[BOP-REG; BOP-NIC]}}{TEMPERATURE / SEASON}{\textit{S. reginae}: 55–65°F nights; outside above 70°F. \textit{S. nicolai}: 65–85°F; protect below 50°F/from cold drafts. Species-specific. \textbf{[BOP-REG; BOP-NIC]}}
\CareRow{FEEDING / MAINTENANCE}{\textit{S. reginae}: feed regularly spring/summer. \textit{S. nicolai}: balanced liquid feed monthly spring/summer. Pause as active growth slows. \textbf{[BOP-REG; BOP-NIC]}}{PROPAGATION}{\textit{S. reginae} only: divide a 4–5-shoot old clump into single stems; replant at original depth and keep moist—not dry or soggy—until rooted. \textbf{[BOP-UF]}}
\CareRow{TROUBLESHOOTING}{Both: root rot can follow excess water/poor drainage. \textit{S. nicolai}: also inspect for wind damage and listed pests. \textbf{[BOP-REG; BOP-NIC]}}{NATURAL HISTORY / TRIVIA}{\textit{S. reginae} is a clumping South African perennial; \textit{S. nicolai} becomes much larger and woody/tree-like. \textbf{[BOP-REG; BOP-NIC]}}
