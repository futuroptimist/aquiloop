\CareRow{LIGHT / EXPOSURE}{Both candidates need bright conditions: sunny indoors for \textit{S. reginae}; bright indirect light for \textit{S. nicolai}. \CareRow{WATER}{Candidate cues differ: water \textit{S. reginae} freely spring/summer and less in winter; water \textit{S. nicolai} when the top layer feels dry. No fixed interval. \textbf{[BOP-REG; BOP-NIC]}}{TEMPERATURE / SEASON}{Candidate guidance: moderate indoor temperatures for \textit{S. reginae}; \textit{S. nicolai} does not tolerate below 50°F or cold drafts. Confirm species/conditions. \textbf{[BOP-REG; BOP-NIC]}}
\CareRow{FEEDING / MAINTENANCE}{Both candidate profiles recommend spring/summer feeding; schedules differ. Defer a schedule until species and active growth are confirmed. \textbf{[BOP-REG; BOP-NIC]}}{PROPAGATION}{\textit{S. reginae} example only: in late spring or early summer, divide a four-to-five-shoot old clump into single-stem divisions; replant at original depth, moist until rooted. Dry or soggy conditions can kill leaves. \textbf{[BOP-UF]}}
\CareRow{TROUBLESHOOTING}{Root rot can follow overwatering/poor drainage; \textit{S. nicolai} also lists wind damage and pests. Inspect conditions and species before diagnosis. \textbf{[BOP-REG; BOP-NIC]}}{NATURAL HISTORY / TRIVIA}{\textit{S. reginae} is a clumping South African perennial; \textit{S. nicolai} becomes much larger and woody/tree-like.
