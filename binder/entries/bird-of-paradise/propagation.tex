\CompanionHeader{PROPAGATION}{Bird of paradise}{Unresolved Strelitzia species}{Indoors initially; S. reginae and S. nicolai are separate candidates, not interchangeable.}
\Context{Division primary / S. reginae protocol only. General guidance; observe parent readiness before acting.}
\begin{minipage}[t]{.625\linewidth}\raggedright\vspace{0pt}
\Step{1}{Resolve applicability}{The specimen is only Strelitzia. This sequence applies to candidate S. reginae; do not transfer its shoot counts, establishment or flowering times to S. nicolai. \textbf{[2,3]}}
\Step{2}{Choose season \& parent}{Use an old reginae clump with four to five shoots in late spring or early summer. This is general parent-readiness guidance, not an assessment of the photographed plant. \textbf{[3]}}
\Step{3}{Allocate shoots and roots}{Separate into single-stem divisions, each retaining a healthy root system. A leaf alone or a rootless stem is not the supported division method. \textbf{[3]}}
\Step{4}{Replant at original depth}{Keep the original soil depth in well-drained soil. The established-plant recipe is a proposed container starting point, not a published division-medium ratio. \textit{Proposed.} \textbf{[1,2,3]}}
\Step{5}{Maintain moisture}{Keep soil moist while roots establish; neither dry nor soggy. Establishment care differs from the drier seasonal care of an established plant. \textbf{[1,3]}}
\Step{6}{Allow establishment time}{Keep moist until roots establish, for at least three months. This is a minimum establishment period, not the date on which every division is ready. \textbf{[3]}}
\Step{7}{Inspect before normal care}{Confirm root establishment before ending establishment moisture care. Yellowing or dying leaves prompt a moisture check; they do not prove a single cause. \textit{Proposed.} \textbf{[3]}}
\Step{8}{Separate flowering from rooting}{Reginae divisions may flower after 1-2 years under suitable culture. Seed-raised reginae may take 3-5 years. Neither interval establishes lifespan. \textbf{[3]}}
\end{minipage}\hfill\begin{minipage}[t]{.345\linewidth}\raggedright\vspace{0pt}
\Note{Gather}{Clean division tool, draining containers and medium; a covered seed container for the alternate.}
\Note{Milestones, not deadlines}{\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{.30\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{.60\linewidth}@{}}\textbf{Reginae seed} & Usually 1-2 months; broad range 1-12 months.\\\textbf{Nicolai} & Seed and division intervals unknown.\\\end{tabular}\par \textbf{[3]}}
\Note{Reginae seed alternate}{Soak fresh seed 1-2 days in lukewarm water, then nick the coat. Sow 0.5-1 in deep in vermiculite; cover, keep damp and warm in indirect light. Pot at two true leaves; larger-pot readiness follows in 2-3 months. \textbf{[3]}}
\Note{Check 1}{Yellowing leaves: check dry or soggy soil; restore moist drainage. \textit{Proposed.} \textbf{[3]}}
\Note{Check 2}{Stalled division: check original depth and moist establishment care. \textit{Proposed.} \textbf{[3]}}
\Note{Check 3}{No seedling: check damp warmth and indirect light; allow the broad range. \textit{Proposed.} \textbf{[3]}}
\end{minipage}\par\vspace{4pt}
