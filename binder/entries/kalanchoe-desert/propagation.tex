\CompanionHeader{PROPAGATION}{Kalanchoe 'Desert Surprise'}{Candidate Kalanchoe humilis 'Desert Surprise'}{Outdoor grow bag; cultivar identity and actual winter protection remain unverified.}
\Context{Stem cutting / spring or summer. General guidance; observe parent readiness before acting.}
\begin{minipage}[t]{.625\linewidth}\vspace{0pt}
\Step{1}{Prepare}{Clean sharp pruners and prepare a small draining container with cactus potting soil or perlite. Keep this temporary rooting medium separate from the established-plant recipe. \textbf{[3]}}
\Step{2}{Select \& cut}{Choose a healthy stem tip; the general extension method uses a 3-6 in cutting. This is general succulent guidance, not a measured requirement for this specimen. \textbf{[3,1]}}
\Step{3}{Expose the lower stem}{Remove leaves from the lower half, retaining healthy upper growth and keeping the cut clean. The lower stem is the part that will enter the medium. \textbf{[3]}}
\Step{4}{Dry \& callus}{Rest the cutting dry on a tray or in an empty cup for several days. Proceed when the cut surface is dry and callused, rather than when a date arrives. \textbf{[3]}}
\Step{5}{Place upright}{Insert the callused lower stem upright in the draining propagation medium. Do not use a glass of water. No numeric insertion depth is established for this candidate. \textbf{[3]}}
\Step{6}{Maintain conditions}{Avoid saturation and standing water. Use bright light without abrupt midday sun. The proposed 50-70 F (10-21 C; C rounded) range adapts general indoor succulent guidance. \textit{Proposed.} \textbf{[2,6,3]}}
\Step{7}{Inspect without uprooting}{Check very gently for anchoring resistance; do not repeatedly lift the cutting. Watch for sustained new growth as well as anchoring. No dependable cultivar-specific root-initiation interval was found. \textit{Proposed.} \textbf{[2,3,1]}}
\Step{8}{Transfer \& acclimate}{Move rooted material to a draining established-plant container. Start outdoor acclimation in semi-shade and increase sun gradually; protect against unsuitable cold. Use observation, not a fixed transplant day. \textbf{[4,6,1]}}
\end{minipage}\hfill\begin{minipage}[t]{.345\linewidth}\vspace{0pt}
\Note{Milestones, not deadlines}{Callus: several days, until dry. Root initiation: unknown. Transfer: anchored roots and sustained new growth; no fixed day count. \textit{Proposed.} \textbf{[3,1]}}
\Note{Plantlet option}{Only if a confirmed candidate actually produces viable attached plantlets. RHS lists their removal; do not infer this behavior from unrelated Kalanchoe species. \textbf{[1]}}
\Note{Check 1}{Soft/dark base: check saturation, drainage and callusing; restart from sound tissue. \textit{Proposed.} \textbf{[2,3]}}
\Note{Check 2}{Shriveling: check overheating or bone-dry medium; correct without saturation. \textit{Proposed.} \textbf{[2,6]}}
\Note{Check 3}{Scorch after transfer: return to semi-shade; resume gradual acclimation. \textit{Proposed.} \textbf{[6]}}
