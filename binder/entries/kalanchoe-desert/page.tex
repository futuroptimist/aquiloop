\CareRow{LIGHT / EXPOSURE}{Candidate: full light indoors; Kalanchoe generally: bright indoor light. Present outdoor exposure is unknown. \textbf{[KAL-RHS; KAL-IA]}}{SOIL / SUBSTRATE}{Starting medium: peat-free, loam-based potting compost with added grit, in a draining container; never leave it standing in water. The sources do not specify proportions. \textbf{[KAL-RHS; KAL-IA]}}
\CareRow{WATER}{Let soil dry between waterings, but not to wilting. Candidate guidance says moderate water, less in winter; this is not a fixed interval. \textbf{[KAL-RHS; KAL-IA]}}{TEMPERATURE / SEASON}{H1b means a 10–15°C absolute-minimum winter band—not a summer growing range. Keep indoors/under glass; if moved out in summer, acclimate gradually to a sunny, sheltered site and return before cold. \textbf{[KAL-HARD; KAL-RHS; KAL-MSU]}}
\CareRow{FEEDING / MAINTENANCE}{Candidate: liquid feed two or three times in the growing season; no pruning required. Adjust to observed growth. \textbf{[KAL-RHS]}}{PROPAGATION}{In spring/summer, take a clean stem section and remove lower leaves. Callus it, then use well-drained medium; an uncallused cutting may rot. \textbf{[KAL-RHS; KAL-PROP]}}
\CareRow{TROUBLESHOOTING}{Inspect for mealybugs, aphids, mildew, and leaf spot. Toxic if eaten by people/pets; keep out of reach and wear gloves if sap irritates skin. \textbf{[KAL-RHS; KAL-NCSU]}}{NATURAL HISTORY / TRIVIA}{RHS describes the candidate as a compact perennial succulent with rounded, fleshy olive-green leaves patterned dark red—not an observation of this specimen. \textbf{[KAL-RHS]}}
