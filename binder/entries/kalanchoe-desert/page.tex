\CareRow{LIGHT / EXPOSURE}{Full indoor light and ventilation. Acclimate before summer outdoors; use a sunny, sheltered site. \textbf{[KAL-RHS; KAL-HARD]}}{SOIL / SUBSTRATE}{Start with peat-free loam-based compost plus grit in a draining pot; never stand it in water. \CareRow{WATER}{Let soil dry, but not to wilting. Water moderately and less as growth slows; avoid fixed intervals. \textbf{[KAL-RHS; KAL-IA]}}{TEMPERATURE / SEASON}{H1b means a UK minimum band of 10–15°C—not a preferred growing range. Keep indoors/under glass; summer outdoors only sunny and sheltered. \textbf{[KAL-RHS; KAL-HARD]}}
\CareRow{FEEDING / MAINTENANCE}{Feed 2–3 times during active growth; no pruning. Toxic if cats/dogs eat it. \textbf{[KAL-RHS; KAL-ASPCA]}}{PROPAGATION}{Spring/summer: remove lower leaves from a clean stem section; callus, then use well-drained medium. Uncallused cuts may rot. \textbf{[KAL-RHS; KAL-PROP]}}
