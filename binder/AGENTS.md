# Binder photographs and layout

- Use only Daniel-owned photographs, with explicit repository/binder permission
  recorded in the asset catalog. No stock substitutes or AI-generated images.
  Do not use generative fill or composites. Ordinary owner-prepared crops and
  color correction are acceptable; preserve the originals outside the repository.
- Missing photographs must occupy their intended frame as clearly labeled draft
  placeholders. Never pass a placeholder off as a specimen photograph.
- Keep the watering guide/log last in every new or revised combined assembly.
- Follow the existing hero convention: square 1:1, 3.30 inches, preferably
  990 x 990 pixels (300 ppi), at least 792 x 792 (240 ppi), no upscaling.
  Prepared sRGB JPEGs belong at `entries/<entry>/assets/overview.jpg`.
  Strip private metadata and screen UI; target 500 KiB, maximum 1 MiB.
- A photo does not establish exact taxonomic identity. Keep provisional identity
  labels until separately reviewed. Do not change existing photo rights metadata
  or replace owner photographs without authorization.
